# NIST CSF 2.0 Remediation Plan

## Purpose

This remediation plan addresses the governance and cybersecurity gaps identified during the simulated NIST CSF 2.0 assessment for CloudNova Technologies.

The goal is to assign corrective actions, ownership, timelines, and validation evidence for identified weaknesses across the Govern, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover functions.

## NIST-RA-001: Formalize Cybersecurity Roles and Responsibilities

### Issue

Cybersecurity responsibilities exist but are not fully documented across departments.

### Corrective Action

Create a formal cybersecurity RACI matrix covering:

- Risk management
- Incident response
- Vendor risk
- Access control
- Vulnerability management
- Security monitoring
- Policy management
- Compliance activities

### Success Criteria

- RACI matrix completed
- Responsible and accountable owners assigned
- Management approval documented
- Matrix reviewed annually

### Validation Evidence

- Approved RACI matrix
- Role descriptions
- Management approval record

### Target Timeline

**60 Days**

---

## NIST-RA-002: Assign Risk Ownership

### Issue

Some High and Medium cybersecurity risks do not have clearly assigned owners.

### Corrective Action

- Review the cybersecurity risk register
- Assign a named owner to every High and Medium risk
- Document treatment responsibility
- Define review dates
- Escalate overdue remediation

### Success Criteria

- 100% of High and Medium risks have assigned owners
- Treatment responsibility documented
- Risk-review dates established

### Validation Evidence

- Updated risk register
- Named risk owners
- Review records

### Target Timeline

**30 Days**

---

## NIST-RA-003: Formalize Risk Acceptance

### Issue

Cybersecurity risk acceptance occurs informally.

### Corrective Action

Create a formal risk acceptance process requiring:

- Risk description
- Inherent risk score
- Treatment decision
- Residual risk
- Business justification
- Compensating controls
- Risk owner
- Management approval
- Expiration or review date

### Success Criteria

- Risk acceptance template approved
- Accepted risks documented
- Management approval retained
- Review dates assigned

### Validation Evidence

- Risk acceptance forms
- Approval records
- Residual-risk documentation

### Target Timeline

**60 Days**

---

## NIST-RA-004: Strengthen Third-Party Risk Governance

### Issue

Vendor security assessments occur, but recurring reassessment is inconsistent.

### Corrective Action

- Create vendor risk classifications
- Require initial security assessments
- Reassess high-risk vendors annually
- Track vendor findings
- Assign vendor risk owners
- Document approval decisions

### Success Criteria

- Critical vendors identified
- High-risk vendors reassessed annually
- Findings tracked to closure
- Approval evidence retained

### Validation Evidence

- Vendor inventory
- Security questionnaires
- Vendor risk assessments
- Reassessment records
- Approval documentation

### Target Timeline

**60 Days**

---

## NIST-RA-005: Establish Annual Cybersecurity Policy Review

### Issue

Cybersecurity policies exist, but annual review evidence is incomplete.

### Corrective Action

Create an annual policy review process documenting:

- Policy owner
- Review date
- Changes made
- Reviewer
- Approver
- Approval date
- Next review date

### Success Criteria

- All critical policies assigned owners
- Annual reviews completed
- Approvals retained
- Outdated policies updated

### Validation Evidence

- Policy review log
- Updated policies
- Approval records

### Target Timeline

**90 Days**

---

## NIST-RA-006: Build a Compliance Obligations Register

### Issue

Compliance obligations and responsible owners are not centrally tracked.

### Corrective Action

Create a compliance register containing:

- Requirement
- Regulatory or contractual source
- Applicable business area
- Control owner
- Evidence required
- Review frequency
- Compliance status

### Success Criteria

- Applicable obligations documented
- Owners assigned
- Review frequencies established
- Status tracked

### Validation Evidence

- Compliance obligations register
- Assigned-owner records
- Review documentation

### Target Timeline

**90 Days**

---

## NIST-RA-007: Perform Quarterly Access Reviews

### Issue

Role-based access controls are implemented, but access reviews are inconsistent.

### Corrective Action

- Generate quarterly user-access reports
- Assign reviewers
- Validate business need
- Remove unnecessary permissions
- Document approvals and exceptions

### Success Criteria

- Quarterly reviews completed
- Unnecessary access removed
- Review approvals documented
- Exceptions tracked

### Validation Evidence

- Access review reports
- Reviewer approvals
- Removed-access records

### Target Timeline

**30 Days**

---

## NIST-RA-008: Enforce Vulnerability Remediation SLAs

### Issue

Some vulnerabilities remain open beyond target remediation periods.

### Corrective Action

Establish remediation targets:

- Critical: 7 Days
- High: 30 Days
- Medium: 60 Days
- Low: 90 Days

Require formal risk acceptance for approved exceptions.

### Success Criteria

- Remediation SLAs documented
- Vulnerability aging tracked
- High-risk findings remediated on time
- Exceptions approved

### Validation Evidence

- Vulnerability reports
- Remediation tickets
- SLA tracking reports
- Risk acceptance records

### Target Timeline

**30 Days**

---

## NIST-RA-009: Review Detection Coverage

### Issue

Detection rules exist, but formal coverage reviews are inconsistent.

### Corrective Action

- Detection rules

- Alert quality
- Coverage gaps

- Monitoring gaps documented
- Missing detections assigned for remediation
- Critical log sources confirmed

### Validation Evidence

- Detection inventory
- Coverage review report
- SIEM screenshots
- Remediation tracking

### Target Timeline

**90 Days**

## NIST-RA-010: Conduct Incident Response Tabletop Exercises

### Issue

Incident response procedures are not tested consistently.

### Corrective Action

Conduct annual tabletop exercises involving:

- Security
- IT
- Management
- Legal
- Communications

Document:

- Scenario
- Participants
- Response actions
- Escalation decisions
- Communication steps
- Lessons learned
- Corrective actions

### Success Criteria

- Exercise completed
- Findings documented
- Corrective actions assigned
- Incident response plan updated

### Validation Evidence

- Tabletop report
- Participant list
- Lessons learned
- Updated incident response plan

### Target Timeline

**60 Days**

---

## NIST-RA-011: Perform Backup Restoration Testing

### Issue

Backups are performed, but restoration testing is inconsistent.

### Corrective Action

- Conduct quarterly restoration tests
- Select representative critical systems
- Record recovery results
- Measure recovery time
- Track failed tests
- Retest after corrective action

### Success Criteria

- Quarterly tests completed
- Critical systems successfully restored
- Recovery results documented
- Failed tests remediated

### Validation Evidence

- Restore test reports
- Recovery logs
- Recovery-time results
- Corrective-action records

### Target Timeline

**90 Days**

---

## Closure Process

A remediation item should only be marked **Closed** after:

1. Corrective action is completed
2. Supporting evidence is collected
3. GRC or security reviewer validates the evidence
4. Remaining risk is documented
5. Closure approval is recorded

---

## Final Goal

The goal of this remediation plan is to strengthen cybersecurity governance and improve alignment with NIST CSF 2.0.

High-risk issues involving risk ownership, third-party governance, access control, vulnerability remediation, and incident response should be addressed first.
