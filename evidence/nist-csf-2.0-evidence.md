# NIST CSF 2.0 Governance Evidence

## Purpose

This folder contains supporting evidence for the simulated NIST CSF 2.0 governance and cybersecurity risk assessment for CloudNova Technologies.

The evidence demonstrates how governance controls, cybersecurity risks, gaps, and remediation activities were evaluated across the NIST CSF 2.0 functions.

## Evidence Included

- NIST CSF governance matrix
- NIST CSF gap assessment
- NIST CSF risk register
- Governance findings
- Remediation plan

## NIST CSF 2.0 Functions Reviewed

- Govern
- Identify
- Protect
- Detect
- Respond
- Recover

## Key Governance Areas

- Cybersecurity policies
- Roles and responsibilities
- Risk ownership
- Risk acceptance
- Third-party risk management
- Compliance oversight
- Access governance
- Vulnerability management
- Incident response
- Recovery testing

## Final Assessment

The assessment identified High and Medium risk gaps that require remediation to improve cybersecurity governance and alignment with NIST CSF 2.0.
